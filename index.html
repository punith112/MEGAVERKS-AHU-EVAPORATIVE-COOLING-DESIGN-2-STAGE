How the air moves

Fresh air · outside to room

  1. Hot outdoor air enters through the louvers into the mixing chamber.
  2. It runs up the dry face of the separation plate, gives its heat to the plate, and rises through the top duct into the room. It never touches water.

Return air · room to room

  1. Room air enters through the end louvers and the fine filter.
  2. The lower half of the cooling pad and the polymer plate HX chill it with water.
  3. The main fan blows the cold air onto the other face of the separation plate, where it takes heat from the fresh air.
  4. It rises through the gap beside the plate, above the baffle, into the upper deck.
  5. The upper half of the HX and pad chill it again.
  6. The secondary fan lifts it into the top box and discharges it to the room.
